INTERFACE CHANGES (role public-rpc)
  - eth_getProof [address, keys, block]
      - keys: an array of at most maxProofKeys (default 128, range 1-1024) hex words of 1-64 digits, with no repeats by
        value.
      - block: EIP-1898 {blockHash, requireCanonical?}, a bare 32-byte hash, a quantity or a tag. "pending" is refused.
      - exactly 3 parameters.
  - eth_getBlockByHash [hash, false]: full transactions are refused; exactly 2 parameters.
  - eth_getTransactionCount [addr, "pending"]: "pending" is accepted for this method ONLY. Balance, call, code,
    storage, proof and logs still refuse it.
  - eth_maxPriorityFeePerGas []: no parameters.
  - Block selectors elsewhere now also accept a bare 32-byte hash. eth_getBlockByNumber stays tags or numbers only.
  - Every answer: X-Enclave-Rpc-Class is one of chain-state, proof-material-unverified (getProof, getBlockByHash),
    mempool-unverified (pending nonce), fee-quote-unverified (tip) or submission. The header is exposed to CORS. Nothing
    is labelled verified by method name.
  - Unchanged:
      - still forbidden by name: eth_createAccessList, eth_sign*, eth_sendTransaction, accounts, txpool, filters,
        admin/debug/personal/wallet;
      - the own-property allowlist;
      - all body, response, deadline, rate and in-flight bounds;
      - the health gate (syncing/stale -> 503);
      - kind null; explicit profile URLs; source and host headers; no hidden fallback;
      - the verifier's methods are unchanged (getProof/getBlockByHash are node material only).
