## the host-side read, at exactly the printed guest-physical addresses (QEMU monitor pmemsave, 4096 bytes each)
private gpa=0x361b2000: 67 full repetitions of "ENCLAVE-MEMMARKER/1 <marker> PRIVATE "
shared  gpa=0x361b3000: 68 full repetitions of "ENCLAVE-MEMMARKER/1 <marker> SHARED "
unmarked control gpa=0x362b2000: marker present = False
=> on an unisolated guest a reader of guest-physical memory finds both pages at their addresses and nothing
   elsewhere: the address-level check works. On Hyper-V the reader is enclave-d1's saved-state decoder
   (vmsavedstatedumpprovider, ReadGuestPhysicalAddress). On type 16 both pages must match (its positive
   control). On a VBS type 1 the SHARED page must still match (it reads THIS VM), and the PRIVATE page is the test.
