ATTRIBUTION of the runs in this directory (written 2026-09-22 after the fact; the logs themselves are unchanged)

w4.0.log, w4-a.log/.caps, w4-b.log/.caps -- NOT int4 runs. EXCLUDED from every int4 quality or performance conclusion.
    The worker ran the int4 graphs (tpu/g5-h4ds-w4), but the VM did not receive the int4 bundle: it reused the int8
    bundle already in its encrypted store (lanes-h4ds, sha256 b9a24410...), because its cache check compared only
    the size and the first 8 bytes, and the int4 bundle (cd56ee87...) has the same size and the same magic. Each log
    says so itself: "TPU bundle already in the VM's encrypted storage (1757 MiB), not streamed". So the VM cancelled
    its pads with the int8 matrix while the TPU multiplied by int4, and the verification counter (104,724 and 104,693
    of 104,960 samples wrong) measured that MISMATCH, not int4 arithmetic. gwcheck + gw_ref.py afterwards showed the
    int4 graphs compute their own integers to 1 LSB (tpu/gwcheck, commit 2b993ba7).
    They remain useful only as evidence of the defect (the bundle cache, and a verifier that counted instead of
    refusing), both fixed in 2b993ba7. w4.0.log is an empty stub: that run's results were lost to logcat rotation.

i8-a.log/.caps, i8-b.log/.caps -- valid int8 controls (int8 graphs, int8 bundle in the VM). 57 tokens at 1.02 and
    (i8-b) similar tok/s; verification 2 and 1 disagreements of 1 LSB. Run on a warm, charging phone whose big-core
    cap fell to 2.09-2.19 GHz mid-run (the .caps files), so the absolute rates are not comparable with cool runs.

i8-c, w4-c, i8-d, w4-d -- the digest-bound rerun (lane-run2.sh with BUNDLE_SHA256); each log names the bundle the
    VM confirmed. These are the only runs here that can speak for int4.
