Check a host’s evidence

Your browser checks a live host's hardware attestation itself, with Enclave's source-available verifier.

Default verification uses Enclave’s measurement allowlist: a successful check is limited. For the confidential-server check, use enclave attest --expect <measurement> with a measurement you derived independently. This removes reliance on the allowlist for that comparison; other trust assumptions remain.

How this works

The page draws a fresh nonce. The relay carries it to the host and brings the host's evidence back untouched, and the verifier (served from this site, hash-pinned) checks it against hardware roots built into it: AMD's for SEV-SNP, Google's for phone pVMs, the TPM maker's for Enclave Shield, where the page also mints the TPM credential itself. The relay can delay a check but cannot fake one. Expected builds come from the operator's list, so a pass reads "genuine and fresh", not "independently reproduced". From a terminal: enclave attest --expect <measurement>.
