Don't take this page's word for it: run the check now

Your browser loads Enclave's own open-source verifier (verifier/, served from this site) and checks any live box you pick itself: it sends the box a fresh nonce and verifies what comes back to a hardware root. An AMD SEV-SNP box's report goes to AMD's root (firmware level, guest policy, launch measurement); a phone pVM's attestation chain goes to Google's root (the published build and runtime); an Enclave Shield host's TPM quote goes to its chip maker's root (Secure Boot on, test signing off, VBS running; the host itself stays trusted). Every host in the Live enclaves list carries the same check, and your dashboard runs it for each deployment's box.

The verifier is served from this site (same-origin, hash-pinned in its manifest). The nonce goes through the relay to the box and the box's evidence comes back untouched, to be checked against the roots built into the verifier (AMD's, with its certificates through the relay's KDS mirror; Google's; the TPM makers'), so the relay can delay a check but cannot fake one. For an Enclave Shield host this page also mints the TPM credential the box must recover, so the relay cannot answer for its TPM. The expected measurements and builds are the operator's own allowlist, which is why a pass here says "genuine and fresh" rather than "independently reproduced". From a terminal: enclave attest, with --expect <measurement> to pin the value yourself.
