Don't take this page's word for it: run the check now

Your browser loads Enclave's own open-source verifier (verifier/, served from this site) and checks a live attested box itself: it sends the box a fresh nonce, then verifies the hardware report it signs up to AMD's root of trust, the firmware level, the guest policy, the binding of the box's key to your nonce, and the launch measurement. Your dashboard runs the same check for each deployment's box.

    The verifier is served from this site (same-origin, hash-pinned in its manifest). The nonce goes through the relay to the box and the box's report comes back untouched; AMD's certificates come through the relay's KDS mirror and are checked against the AMD root built into the verifier, so the relay can delay a check but cannot fake one. The expected measurement is the operator's own allowlist, which is why a pass here says "genuine and fresh" rather than "independently reproduced". From a terminal: enclave attest, with --expect <measurement> to pin the value yourself.
