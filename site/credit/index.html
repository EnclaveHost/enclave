Your Enclave credit, directly on chain

Your credit lives in a vault contract that only your passkey can spend from. This page reads that vault from chain endpoints you choose and has your passkey sign each operation here. No Enclave account, server or API is used. Enclave's hosted checkout and dashboard keep working as before.

  • Your passkey works only on this site (). That is how passkeys work, and the vault accepts no other site.
  • Credit pays for Enclave runtime only. It cannot pay transaction fees: you choose who pays the gas, in ETH (your wallet, or a relay that offers to).
  • To add credit by card you still need Enclave's checkout, since a card payment needs a processor. Nothing here sells or moves credit on its own.
  • Prepaid lease gas is not available for apps your vault owns. The gas reserve only accepts settings from the owner's own transaction, which a vault cannot send.
